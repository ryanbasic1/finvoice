What is FinVoice?

FinVoice lets you add expenses using natural speech like “₹500 for groceries yesterday”, auto-categorizes them, and shows monthly insights. It also supports Hinglish, continuous voice mode (Bot), and PDF summaries.

Voice Expenses

Single-tap voice input and a continuous “Bot” to capture multiple expenses hands‑free.

Hinglish Parsing

Understands common Hinglish patterns like “do hazaar rent” or “Rs 200 chai”.

Insights & PDF

Monthly summary, category breakdown, and one‑click PDF export.

Goals / SIP

Calculate monthly SIP needed to reach your financial goals.

Online Payments

Quick view of UPI wallets like GPay, PhonePe, Paytm.

Advisory

Headlines & signals with “Invest / Hold / Avoid” guidance (demo, rules‑based).

How to get started

  1. Login to the demo account.
  2. Go to Dashboard and click “Voice Input” or type an expense.
  3. Open “Insights” for monthly summary, or “Goal Calculator” for SIP.
